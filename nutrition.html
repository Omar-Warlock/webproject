Nutrition Tips

Fueling your body with the right nutrients is the cornerstone of a vibrant student life. Discover simple, actionable advice to balance your plate and boost your energy levels for both study and play.

Fruits & Veggies

Aim for five portions a day to get a variety of essential vitamins and minerals.

Protein

Essential for muscle repair and cognitive function. Include beans, eggs, or lean meats.

Whole Grains

Source of steady energy. Choose brown rice, oats, and whole-wheat bread.

Hydration

Stay hydrated to maintain focus. Aim for 2 liters of water daily for optimal health.
